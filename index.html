Purla Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Purla scans product barcodes and ingredient labels and shows you information about what's in them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Purla is operated from the United States.
Contact: getpurla@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account we collect your email address and store an account identifier. We use Google Firebase Authentication for this.
  • Your scan history. When you scan a product we store a record of it — the product, the score we calculated, and when you scanned it — so you can see it again later. This is linked to your account.
  • Photographs you take. When a product isn't in any database, you can photograph its ingredients label so we can read it. You can also add a photo of the front of the pack.
  • Barcodes you scan. We send the barcode to product databases to look up the product.
  • Technical information. Our servers keep short-lived logs containing IP addresses and request details, used to operate the service and prevent abuse.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, payment details, contacts, location, or health information.

How we use photographs — please read this part

Ingredients photographs are sent to Anthropic, an AI provider, to read the text on the label. We do not keep the photograph after reading it. We keep the ingredient text.

Front-of-pack photographs are stored and shared with all users of Purla. If you add a photo of a product, other people who scan that product will see your photo. Only add photos of the product itself. Do not include yourself, other people, or anything private in the frame.

If you later delete your account, product photographs you contributed remain in the shared product database, but are no longer linked to you. This is because other people rely on that product information. If you want a specific photograph removed, contact us.

Anyone can report a photograph they think is wrong or inappropriate, and reported photographs are hidden.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Purla contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.

We use these providers to run the service:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database; stores your account and scan history.
  • Railway — hosting; runs our servers.
  • Anthropic — AI; reads ingredient photographs and writes product summaries.
  • Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts — open product databases we query by barcode.

Where product information comes from

Product data comes from Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, open databases maintained by contributors worldwide, and from ingredient labels read from photographs. Ingredient assessments are based on European Union cosmetic regulation and published scientific assessments.

Purla is not medical advice. It reports what is on a label and what regulators say about it. Whether a product is suitable for you depends on things a label cannot tell us.

How long we keep things

  • Your account and scan history are kept until you delete your account.
  • Cached product information is kept for up to 30 days and then refreshed.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period for operational purposes.
  • Data may persist in backups for a limited period after deletion.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Edit Profile screen in the app. This permanently deletes your account, your scan history, and your personal data. It cannot be undone.

Product photographs you contributed stay in the shared database, with your identity removed, as described above.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond.

Children

Purla is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
