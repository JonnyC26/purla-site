A product scored wrong, or the ingredients look wrong

Email us the product name and barcode. Purla reads ingredients from Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, and from photographs of labels, so an error usually comes from one of those sources and we can correct it.

A product photo is wrong or inappropriate

Tap Report photo underneath the product image in the app. Reported photos are hidden.

A product isn't found

Tap Photograph the ingredients and take a picture of the ingredients panel. Purla reads the label directly, and the product is then available to everyone.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Profile, then Edit Profile, and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your scan history and your personal data. It cannot be undone.
