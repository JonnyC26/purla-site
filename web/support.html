Purla Support

Questions, problems, or something scored wrong?

getpurla@gmail.com

We read everything and usually reply within a few days.

A product scored wrong, or the ingredients look wrong

Email us the product name and barcode. Purla reads ingredients from USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, and from photographs of labels, so an error usually comes from one of those sources and we can correct it.

A product photo is wrong or inappropriate

If the photo was added by a Purla user, tap Report photo under the product name. Reported photos are hidden. For any other photo, email us the barcode.

A product isn't found

Tap Scan Food Label and take a picture of the ingredients panel. Purla reads the ingredients from the label. To get a 0–100 score, tap Add nutrition facts and photograph the Nutrition Facts panel. A score from your photo is shown only to you. Labels measured in mL (most drinks) aren't supported yet.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Profile, then Edit Profile, and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your scan history and your personal data. It cannot be undone.
